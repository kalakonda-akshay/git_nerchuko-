FAQ

Is this real Git?
No. Git Nerchuko is a safe, interactive client-side simulator that teaches the same real commands and concepts without touching your local filesystem.
Git vs GitHub?
Git tracks version history on your local machine. GitHub hosts repositories online and adds pull requests, issues, and code reviews.
How do I earn and spend XP?
Complete missions in the Playground to earn XP. Fewer errors give more bonus stars. Spend your XP in the Shop on custom terminal CRT themes and tactile audio effects.
Where is my progress stored?
All your progress, XP, and settings are saved in your browser's localStorage. Clearing site data resets it.
